# ResQ: User Stories and Product Backlog (v6, final)

Group 30. This is the mid-evaluation workbook *DRCH_User_Stories_SRS_Final_v5.xlsx* (119 stories, 16 epics) brought up to date with what was built. It adds the stories for work that came after the mid-evaluation, which gives **138 stories in 18 epics**. Each story is written as a card: the front holds the user story, and the back holds the acceptance criteria. Requirement IDs refer to [01-requirements-srs-v2.md](01-requirements-srs-v2.md); sprint codes refer to [04-sprint-details.md](04-sprint-details.md).

**Status:** **Done** means implemented and deployed. **Partial** means the core is done and the gap is in the note and in [08-requirements-not-implemented.md](08-requirements-not-implemented.md). **Backlog** means not built.

**Planned** is the sprint given at mid-evaluation, when five two-week sprints S1–S5 were planned. **Delivered** is the sprint in which the story was actually completed: S0 (11 Sep – 29 Oct), S1 (30 Oct – 4 Nov), S2 (7 – 12 Nov) or S3 (15 – 22 Nov).
